Your daily 3 wins

Tick 3 boxes. That's the whole day.

Fuel

One simple nutrition habit, like a protein-first breakfast, or swapping one sugary drink for water. You'll get the day's tip on WhatsApp each morning.

Move

20 minutes, your way: a walk after dinner, a home workout, a run before the heat. Something always beats nothing.

Check in

Drop a quick photo or a ✅ in the group. Takes 10 seconds and keeps you going, and lifts everyone else too.

No scores, no leaderboards. Just you, getting a little better each day, with a crew cheering you on.

The 3 weeks
Week 1

Reset

Clean up the basics: water, protein, sleep, and getting moving every day.

Week 2

Build

Stack the habits. A bit more movement, smarter food swaps, the 5am crew if you're brave.

Week 3

Own it

Make it stick. Find what fits your real life, then finish strong together.

Recognition, not rankings

Show up. Lift others. Get noticed.

There's no winner to beat. Mazen will spotlight the people who show up, share honestly and lift the group, and they'll take home a Meta Mov goodie bag.

  • Weekly shout-outs in the group and on @meta_mov
  • Most consistent, most inspiring and best supporter

Questions
Is it really free?

Yes. The first round of the challenge is completely free.

I'm not fit. Can I still join?

That's exactly who it's for. Every win can be scaled to where you are today.

How much time does it take?

About 20–30 minutes a day, and you choose when.

Do I need a gym?

No. Walking, home workouts or the gym all count.
